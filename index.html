What is the tool

A free online tool with four jobs: it shows how far nature-based solutions (NBS) are integrated in a specific area, suggests NBS that fit local climate risks, assesses their potential to reduce those risks, and estimates their costs. It is meant for partners, public authorities and other organisations that plan or implement NBS.

Why it matters

NBS can reduce heat, flooding and drought in Mediterranean cities, but their wider adoption is held back by knowledge gaps, limited involvement of local stakeholders and little experience in planning and coordinating them. The assessment shows where you are strong and where the gaps are, so you can plan fit-for-purpose NBS before committing resources.

Developed by the University of Jordan within NBS4MED (Nature based solutions for climate resilient Mediterranean cities), co-funded by the European Union through the Interreg NEXT MED Programme. Project partners test this version and their feedback shapes the refined one.

How it works

  1. 1

    Start

    Open the tool. No account or login is needed.

  2. 2

    Answer

    Describe your site, pick climate risks, then score 14 indicators from 0 to 2.

  3. 3

    Get results

    See your level, strengths and gaps, then NBS options that suit your risks.

  4. 4

    Download & improve

    Save a PDF or CSV report, adjust answers and run it again.
